Why event tagging matters
Months with sale events (Great Indian Festival, Diwali, Prime Day) have abnormally high page views that inflate the weighted average baseline. Tagging them tells the prediction engine to skip those months when calculating the baseline — so predictions are based on normal-traffic months only. The seasonality multiplier still captures the year-on-year trend using the months around the event.

Total page views ÷ days in month
Sum of all ASIN page views for the month ÷ number of days = true average daily page views · event months highlighted
Formula: Σ(all ASIN page views) ÷ days in month · not an average of ASIN averages
Weighted CVR — units ÷ total page views
Portfolio conversion rate = total units sold ÷ total page views · weighted by traffic, not a simple average across ASINs
Formula: Σ(units) ÷ Σ(page views) · high-traffic ASINs weighted more than low-traffic ones

Monthly upload
Add a new month — existing history is preserved · conflict detection built in
Step 1 — Download from Amazon Seller Central
Do this at the start of each month for the previous month
1
Sign in to Seller Central
Go to sellercentral.amazon.in and log in with your seller credentials.
2
Open Business Reports
Top menu → Reports → Business Reports
3
Select the right report
Left sidebar → By ASIN → Detail Page Sales and Traffic by Child Item
4
Set the date range
Set From and To to the full calendar month — e.g. 01 Mar 2026 → 31 Mar 2026. Set unit to Monthly.
5
Download as CSV
Click Download (.csv). File saves as e.g. BusinessReport-03-2026.csv
💡
Best practice
Download on the 1st–3rd of each month for the previous month. Amazon adjusts numbers 1–2 days after month end — waiting until the 3rd ensures the data is finalised.
Step 2 — Upload to ListLens
Select the CSV you just downloaded from Seller Central
Drop CSV here or click to browse
